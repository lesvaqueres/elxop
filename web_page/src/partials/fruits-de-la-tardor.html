Trees and shrubs

Olive

Olea europaea

The tree of the Mediterranean. Silver-backed leaves, a slow, twisting trunk, and small fruit that ripens from green to black in autumn.

Ginkgo

Ginkgo biloba

One of the oldest tree species on earth. Its fan-shaped leaves turn a clear butter yellow in autumn before falling almost all at once.

Fig

Ficus carica

Several smooth grey trunks that open out like a hand, and branches that spread over the pergola. Underneath it the shade is cool, and at the end of summer the figs ripen purple among the big leaves.

Hawthorn

Crataegus monogyna

A thorny hedgerow tree with white blossom in spring and small red haws in autumn that the birds come for.

Quince

Cydonia oblonga

Golden, fuzzy fruit with a strong perfume. Too hard to eat raw, but cooked slowly it becomes membrillo.

Pomegranate

Punica granatum

In autumn the leaves turn yellow and the fruit splits open on the branch, showing the red seeds inside.

Persimmon

Diospyros kaki

Bright orange fruit that stays on the bare branches after the leaves have fallen, like lanterns into winter.

Strawberry tree

Arbutus unedo

Flowers and ripe fruit at the same time: small white bells beside round red berries with a grainy skin.

Feijoa

Acca sellowiana

An evergreen shrub with grey-green leaves and red-and-white flowers. Its green fruit tastes somewhere between pineapple and guava.

Dog rose

Rosa canina

The wild rose of hedges and paths. Simple pale pink flowers, then red rose hips that last into winter.

Goji berry

Lycium barbarum

A loose, arching shrub with small purple flowers and bright red berries.

Raspberry

Rubus idaeus

Canes that fruit along the edge of the garden; when the berries are ripe they come away easily from the stem.

Herbs and small plants

Rosemary

Salvia rosmarinus

Needle-like leaves that smell of the Mediterranean hills when you brush past, and small blue flowers the bees love.

Lemon balm

Melissa officinalis

Soft, crinkled leaves with a clean lemon scent. A classic for calming infusions.

Water mint

Mentha aquatica

A mint that looks for wet ground. Round lilac flower heads and a strong, fresh smell.

Rue

Ruta graveolens

Blue-grey, rounded leaves with a sharp, bitter smell. A traditional garden herb, better looked at than touched.

Yarrow

Achillea millefolium

Feathery leaves and flat clusters of tiny white flowers. A tough herb that flowers for a long time.

Wood sorrel

Oxalis

Three heart-shaped leaflets, like clover, that fold up in the evening. The leaves taste pleasantly sour.

Purple shamrock

Oxalis triangularis

Deep purple, triangular leaves that open in the light and close at night.

Chayote

Sechium edule

A climbing vine from the squash family. Its pale green, wrinkled fruit hangs from the tendrils in autumn.

Autumn fruits and plants

Nuts

A bowl of almonds, walnuts and hazelnuts, and the tree each one grows on.

Medlar

Mespilus germanica

A small, crooked tree with large leaves. Its brown fruit, open like a crown at the tip, is only eaten once it softens after the first frosts, when it tastes like apple butter.
