Bibliography

At El Xop there are hand-made books that explain its corners: the sky from the viewpoint, the symbols in the labyrinth, the story of labyrinths, and the Way of St James. You can read them here in full.

Photo album The spaces of El Xop A hand-drawn plan of the whole estate and a photographic walk through its corners, ending at the great black poplar that gives it its name. Digital book · 9 pages Astronomy underfoot The Sky Viewpoint A mosaic floor for reading the sky: constellations, sunrise at the solstices, the rainbow, the Milky Way, the winds and the phases of the moon. Digital book · 10 pages The labyrinth of life and its symbols The Plane-Tree Walk A walk in the shade of plane trees that hides “the labyrinth of life”: a path of crossroads with ancient symbols and reflexology circles to walk barefoot. Digital book · 11 pages The seven-circuit labyrinth Mother Earth What a labyrinth is, how to draw a seven-circuit one, what they were for, and where they are found around the world: from Galicia to Nazca, Crete to Arizona. Digital book · 13 pages From Grenyana to Butsènit, and the Game of the Goose The Way of St James El Xop lies right on the Way of St James. A walk between two hermitages, the legend of Lleida's lanterns, the Horta board game and the mystery of the Game of the Goose. Digital book · 15 pages
