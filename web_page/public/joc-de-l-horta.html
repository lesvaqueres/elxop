The Horta Game

This Horta game is an adaptation of the classic “Game of the Goose”. Here the main character is not a goose but a rooster: Coc.

Download the board

How to play

It is a board game for two to four players.

Players take turns to roll one die and move forward as many squares as the points rolled. A player who reaches square 63 with the exact number of points (none left over) has completed the route and wins. A player who arrives with points left over counts that many squares back from 63.

Special squares

There are special squares to watch out for (outlined in red).

  • If you land on a Coc, move on to the next Coc and roll again: “from Coc to Coc, and I roll once more”
  • If you land on square 6, go to 12; if you land on square 12, go to 6: “from canal to ditch I go in one leap”
  • If you land on square 19, you miss three turns: “el Xop, house of nature, halts you on your way”
  • If you land on square 26, go to 53: “with good rain, rise”
  • If you land on square 31, you stay there until another player lands on it: “in the deep well, wait for the light”
  • If you land on square 52, you miss three turns: “along the paths of the Horta I take a stroll”
  • If you land on square 53, go to 26: “hail, however small, spoils the harvest”
  • If you land on 57, you start again: “the lifeless tree makes you start the game again”
