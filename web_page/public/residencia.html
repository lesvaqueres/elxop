Les Vaqueres Artist residency

A refuge in the Horta de Lleida
to listen, create and be.

We live in a world that moves too fast. And sometimes all we need is time and silence.

At the Amics de la Natura association, we are opening the doors of Les Vaqueres, a place made for concentration, creativity and wellbeing, in the middle of nature.

Five welcoming, self-contained apartments (one of them fully accessible) in the quiet landscape of the Horta de Lleida, where the evening light and the rhythm of the land set the pace.

  • You are an artist looking for inspiration.
  • You are studying for an exam.
  • You are writing the first pages of a story.
  • Or you simply need a few days of calm and switching off.

This is your place.

A place to rest.
A breather for your mind.
A place where nature keeps you company.

General conditions of stay

  1. Before arriving

    Artists are asked to:

    • Send a short summary of the project.
    • Say how long they need to stay.

  2. Before leaving

    Artists show a sample of the final result.

    Artists do not need to arrive with a finished work.

Residents share with people from the area:

  • an exhibition
  • a scene
  • a reading
  • some photographs
  • a conversation
  • a glimpse of the process
  • a workshop
  • an activity with local artists

The aim is to connect the residency with the Horta and the land around it.

During the stay: possibilities

Everyday life is part of the residency. A shared house lets you have a space of your own while staying in touch with the others.

Each artist has their own private space

… and shares the common spaces with the others

  • a kitchen
  • a dining and living room
  • the laundry area
  • the outdoor spaces

Each artist can focus on their own project, but can also choose to share part of their process with the other residents.

So resident artists who wish to can propose activities, gatherings or workshops related to their practice, or open them to artists and people from the area too.

Shared residencies: the creation labs

  1. 1

    One week, different disciplines: “sharing know-how”

    5 artists · different disciplines · one place · one time

    For example: Creation week · body, space and image. Those taking part might be:

    • an actor
    • a photographer
    • a set designer
    • a musician
    • an illustrator

    Each develops their own project, but they share daily life and the common spaces. There is no set theme: the richness lies precisely in the diversity.

  2. 2

    One week, one discipline

    Several specialities around a common thread: 5 residents, each with a speciality within the same theme.

    Example: Theatre week · “from idea to stage”

    • acting
    • directing
    • staging
    • costume
    • make-up and character design
    • set design
    • lighting
  3. 3

    And there is a third, especially interesting option

    One resident leads and coordinates the week.

    For example, a theatre director might propose:

    “I want to spend a week researching how a character is built”

    She could invite or suggest different profiles:

    • actor
    • playwright
    • costume designer
    • make-up artist
    • set designer
Option 1
→ the diversity of the artists is what drives it.
Options 2 and 3
→ a shared theme or discipline drives it.

The camera obscura

A camera obscura is an optical device that projects an upside-down image of the outside into a closed space, while photographic developing is the chemical process that makes that image visible and fixes it.

Our camera obscura is inside a van, which is also set up for developing photos.

How stays and workshops are funded

The idea and identity of the project

The association promotes a cultural, creative model. It is a cultural project with a stay. We do not rent out apartments. We offer a place to live, create and share for a while.

Through the “Artist residencies” project, people can join through the different options and make a contribution to take part in the programme and help sustain the place.

On fees, one thing matters: being a non-profit association does not mean it need not be financially sustainable. Quite the opposite. It must bring in enough for maintenance, repairs, cleaning, insurance, utilities, management, communication, renewing equipment, etc.

Any surplus is not shared among owners: it is reinvested in the project.
